About Utech Club

Utech Club exists to develop Muslim leaders who are grounded in Islamic principles and equipped for modern domains and AI.

Mission

Utech Club develops the Muslim leaders of tomorrow, with expertise not only in religious knowledge but in modern domains and AI, so they can lead in the era of AI grounded in Islamic principles.

Approach

We blend faith-based grounding, practical leadership development, and technology literacy so participants can lead communities and organizations with confidence.
